Add it to your website

Paste this line before </body> on every page where the character should appear:

The widget follows the page language automatically: Arabic pages (<html lang="ar"> or a URL containing /ar/) get the Arabic widget and an Arabic greeting.

Options

  • data-lang="ar" or "en" fixes the language instead of following the page.
  • data-position="left" puts the character in the bottom-left corner.
  • data-greeting="off" hides the "Talk to Hamdan" bubble.
  • Any button on your site can open the widget with onclick="AjmanDofVoice.open()".
